## 2. Solar for the plant's own power — what is known
- Utility benchmark: CERC approved **₹4.01/kWh** for a 100 MW solar + 40 MW/120 MWh battery project at Rajnandgaon, CG ([Mercom](https://subscription.mercomindia.com/cerc-approves-tariff-seci-solar-plus-storage-project-chhattisgarh)). CSERC draft generic solar tariff FY26-27: **₹3.40/kWh** for 0.5–2 MW ([SolarQuarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/05/14/chhattisgarh-state-electricity-regulatory-commission-proposes-renewable-energy-generic-tariffs-for-fy-2026-27/amp/)). Neither is a delivered price to a factory.
- If power is **wheeled from a remote solar plant**: add wheeling ₹0.29/kWh (33 kV) or ₹0.85 (below 33 kV), cross-subsidy surcharge ₹0.94–1.40/kWh, captive parallel-operation ₹0.15/kWh; the old 500 MW exemption applied only to projects commissioned by 27 Dec 2023, so assume it does not apply; standby charge ₹10.53/kWh up to contract demand ([Mercom](https://mercomindia.com/chhattisgarh-hikes-fy-2026-electricity-tariffs-by-1-89-across-categories) · [Mercom](https://mercomindia.com/chhattisgarhs-open-access-solar-surge-with-policy)).
- Reading: **on-site (behind-the-meter) solar avoids wheeling and the surcharge**; a remote plant must clear roughly ₹1.4–2.3/kWh of charges before it beats the grid. The plant's HT industrial tariff, which sets the saving, is **[UNVERIFIED]** — read it off your Kharsia/Raigarh electricity bill. No 2026 C&I solar-plus-storage price for Chhattisgarh was found; get 2–3 quotes.
