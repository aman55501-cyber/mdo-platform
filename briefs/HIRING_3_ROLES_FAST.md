## 1. Tender Specialist
**Job:** find, read, bid and track coal-sector tenders — washery operation, RCR, coal supply (ROM and washed) — for VWLR. Reads NITs, checks eligibility against our criteria, prepares EMD / bid documents, tracks results. Works from the Tender247 feed we already receive.
**Must have:** 2+ years preparing bids on CIL/SECL/NTPC/GeM-type portals; reads eligibility clauses (turnover, experience, EMD); e-tender portals (CPP, GeM, NIC); Excel; clear English and Hindi.
**Good to have:** coal logistics or mining contractor background; rate-analysis skills.
**Where they are:** tender consultancies and bid-processing firms in Raipur / Bilaspur / Nagpur / Kolkata; contractors' bid desks at coal-transport and mining firms; people who worked at Tender247-style services.
**20-minute test:** "Here is a real NIT page (we send one). In 10 lines: who can bid, what EMD, what is the deadline, three risks, and your go/no-go." Scored 0–5 on eligibility read, risk spotting, clarity.

## 2. Lead Accountant
**Job:** run books for a multi-entity group (20 entities, 5 individuals), Tally, GST / TDS, monthly closing, bank reconciliations, statement requests for CA Vimal Agrawal & Co, MIS to Aman. Keeps the MISSING_DOCS checklist at zero.
**Must have:** 5+ years, Tally Prime, GST returns, TDS, bank reconciliation, month-end close, handled more than one entity; coordination with a CA firm.
**Good to have:** coal or logistics or hotel accounting; Excel pivot skills.
**Where they are:** mid-size trading and logistics firms and CA-firm staff in Raigarh / Korba / Bilaspur / Raipur.
**20-minute test:** "Five bank-statement lines and five ledger lines (we send): reconcile, name two things you would query, and say which GST / TDS treatment you would check." Scored on accuracy and the queries raised.

## 3. Civil Engineer (washery civil repair and maintenance)
**Job:** repair and maintenance of washery civil structures, foundations, RCC, plant floors, siding works; BOQ, measurement books, contractor billing, site supervision. Reports to the siding and operations lead.
**Must have:** B.E./Diploma Civil, 3+ years on industrial or mining sites, RCC and steel-structure repair, measurement and billing; willing to be at Raigarh site.
**Good to have:** coal washery or railway-siding experience (RITES / SECL contractors); AutoCAD.
**Where they are:** contractors working with SECL, rail siding and thermal-plant civil teams; LinkedIn civil profiles in Raigarh / Korba / Bilaspur; the siding names on your watchlist can refer people.
**20-minute test:** "A washery plant floor slab has cracked (photo we send). Your repair method in 8 lines, the materials list, and the checks before handing it back to operations."
