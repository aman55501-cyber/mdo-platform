# Group Finance Controller: JD, band, process

Scope confirmed by Aman: whole group (all entities), Raipur-based office, reports to Aman.

## Role
Own books and compliance for every entity in the group; coordinate GST/TDS/ROC/ITR with Vimal Agrawal & Co (CA); bank reconciliation across all accounts;
monthly MIS and cash view; inter-company tracking; fixed-asset and capex tracking (washery infra, hotel, solar); prepare payment batches.
Does NOT release payments or sign regulator filings alone: Aman clicks money; CA files.

## Must have
CA (qualified), 5-7 years post-qualification in at least two industries, handled a multi-entity group (5+ entities) with inter-company flows,
GST + TDS + Companies Act hands-on, Tally/ERP fluency, comfortable with WhatsApp/Drive-based workflows, willing to be Raipur-based with site visits to Kharsia/Raigarh.
Nice: manufacturing/mining/logistics, hospitality or real estate exposure, audit-firm background.

## Process
1. Referral first: Vimal ji's CA network (cheapest, pre-vetted). 2. Post on the ICAI job portal and LinkedIn (applications come to you). 3. Screen against the must-haves; prefer group experience over a Big-4 specialist.
4. Paid 2-hour test on dummy data (below). 5. Two employer reference calls. 6. Offer with a 3-month review.
I will not compile named profiles of private individuals from the internet. Candidates come through the channels above; I screen what applies.

## Paid test (dummy data; make the files before the test)
A. Bank reconciliation: a 60-line statement vs ledger with 6 planted differences (timing, duplicate, wrong amount, missing receipt, bank charge, reversed entry).
B. GST: a GSTR-2B vs purchase register with 5 mismatches; ask for ITC at risk and what to chase.
C. Inter-company: two entities, a loan and a service charge booked on one side only; ask for the corrected entries and elimination.
D. 10 lines of questions: TDS on contractor payments, when a lapse is a notice risk, what he would report to the owner weekly.
Score: accuracy 50, speed 20, clarity of write-up 20, questions he asks 10. Pass line decided before you see names.
