## Method: free stuck capital, fund the right gaps
For every physical asset fill one row: book value | realistic market value | annual income it earns | annual carrying cost (interest, tax, upkeep) | what it needs to earn.
Then one decision per asset:
- SELL when carrying cost exceeds income and no catalyst within 12 months (the 12 vehicles; idle land with clean title).
- JV / development agreement when land is good but you lack capital or time (landowner share model).
- LEASE or MANAGEMENT CONTRACT when operations are weak but the asset is good (hotel: that is what the Guptasons meeting is about).
- FIX then hold when a small spend turns a loss into profit (washery infra completion: it unlocks throughput).
- HOLD when income covers carrying cost.
Funding order (highest return per rupee first): 1) finish washery infrastructure so it earns; 2) cut recurring losses (hotel structure); 3) solar (cuts a known recurring cost); 4) BESS only after a study; 5) land/real estate monetisation funds 1-3.
Do not infuse into a loss-maker without a stated break-even and a date.
