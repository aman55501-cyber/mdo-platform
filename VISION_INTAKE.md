# Vision intake — the half hour

Purpose: turn Aman's picture of his ideal life into objectives the fleet can run toward.
Nothing here is an objective until Aman writes it into the Objectives sheet himself.
The Chief of Staff asks; Aman answers; the CoS proposes the diff; Aman confirms.

Rules for the session
- One answer per question. Numbers only where Aman has them; otherwise "unknown, source = X".
- Every answer gets a source or is marked [UNVERIFIED].
- The CoS never fills a blank on Aman's behalf.

## 1. The morning (5 min)
1. Describe the ideal weekday morning in one paragraph: where you are, what you open, what has already run.
2. What is the one surface you want to open? (WhatsApp / app / sheet)
3. How many decisions a day are you willing to click? (today's ask: ___)

## 2. Horizon (5 min)
4. 12-month picture, per entity, one line each: VWLR · Hotel ANS · Dadu Developers · Dadu Builders · Raigarh Land Venture · Rukmani Infrastructure · Aditi Investments · Eureka · MD's Office.
5. 3-year picture, group level: what exists then that does not exist now?
6. What do you want to stop doing personally within 6 months?

## 3. Money (5 min)
7. Which number do you check first each day, and where does it live?
8. Target monthly free cash at group level, and by when. Source for today's figure: ___
9. Debt: what must be gone by when? Source: CFO package / bank files.
10. Capital you are willing to deploy this year, by entity.

## 4. Sales (5 min)
11. Where does the next ₹1 crore of revenue come from, by entity?
12. Which tenders or customers are must-win in the next 90 days?
13. What is a lost sale in your business, and how would the bots recognise one in WhatsApp?

## 5. People and bottlenecks (5 min)
14. Who are the five people whose response time decides your week?
15. What decision gets stuck most often, and where?
16. What would you delegate tomorrow if the report-back were guaranteed?

## 6. Boundaries (5 min)
17. Which chats or topics are off-limits to every bot, no exceptions?
18. What may a bot never say to anyone outside the group?
19. Spend cap confirmation for the fleet (currently set in .env): keep / change.
20. Anything a bot proposed recently that you want killed?

## Output of the session
- Proposed Objectives diff for the sheet (status, owner bot, success metric, review date).
- Proposed per-bot charter changes in fleet.yaml.
- Proposed vision rows for the business register, each with a source.
Aman confirms each line. The CoS writes nothing to memory before that.
